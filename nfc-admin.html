How to write a player card
1 Find the player. 2 On a supported Android phone, tap Write Card and then touch the NFC tag to the phone. 2 Otherwise tap Copy Link, paste it into your NFC writing app as a Website/URL record, and write the card there. 3 Tap Open Player Page or scan the card to confirm it opens the correct player.
